01 · Introduction

Brazil at a glance

Brazil is the largest country in South America and the fifth-largest country in the world by area. It is known for its enormous natural landscapes, rich biodiversity and long Atlantic coastline.

02 · Physical geography

Landforms & regions

Brazil has a varied landscape made up of plateaus, plains, mountains, valleys and low-lying wetlands.

🌳

Amazon Basin

A vast lowland area in northern Brazil. It contains much of the Amazon Rainforest and is crossed by the Amazon River and its tributaries.

⛰️

Brazilian Highlands

Extensive elevated areas covering much of central, eastern and southern Brazil. They include plateaus, hills and mountain ranges.

🌾

Pantanal

A huge tropical wetland in western Brazil. Seasonal flooding creates habitats for many birds, fish, reptiles and mammals.

🏖️

Coastal plains

Low-lying areas stretch along parts of Brazil's Atlantic coast, with sandy beaches, lagoons, bays and important cities.

03 · Water geography

Rivers & waterways

Brazil has one of the world's largest networks of rivers. These waterways support wildlife, transport, farming, fishing and hydroelectric power.

💧

Amazon River

One of the world's largest rivers by water flow. It travels across northern South America and empties into the Atlantic Ocean.

🌊

Paraná River

A major river in southern South America. It forms part of Brazil's river network and is important for hydroelectricity and transport.

🐟

São Francisco River

An important river that flows through eastern Brazil and provides water for communities, agriculture and ecosystems.

🌿

River tributaries

Smaller rivers and streams join larger rivers, creating connected drainage systems across Brazil's enormous territory.

Geography fact: The Amazon River system carries an extraordinary volume of freshwater into the Atlantic Ocean.
04 · Weather & ecosystems

Climate & biomes

Because Brazil is so large, it has several climate types and natural environments. Rainfall, temperature and vegetation vary from region to region.

🌧️

Equatorial climate

Found mainly in the Amazon region. It is hot and humid, with heavy rainfall through much of the year.

☀️

Tropical climate

Common across central Brazil, often with a rainy season and a drier season.

🌵

Caatinga

A dry region in the northeast, with drought-resistant plants adapted to irregular rainfall.

🌲

Atlantic Forest

A biodiverse forest that once covered much of the eastern coast. Today, only parts of its original area remain.

🦜

Cerrado

A tropical savanna with grasslands, shrubs and scattered trees. It has many unique plant and animal species.

🐆

Pantanal wetlands

A seasonal wetland ecosystem famous for its wildlife and wide floodplains.

05 · Places to know

Important geographical places

These places show the variety of Brazil's physical and human geography.

🌿

Amazon Rainforest

The world's largest tropical rainforest, mostly located in the Amazon Basin. It plays an important role in global biodiversity and the water cycle.

💦

Iguaçu Falls

A spectacular system of waterfalls on the border between Brazil and Argentina, surrounded by subtropical forest.

🏙️

Rio de Janeiro

A coastal city known for its bay, beaches and dramatic granite mountains, including Sugarloaf Mountain.

🌆

Brasília

Brazil's planned capital, located inland on the Central Plateau rather than on the Atlantic coast.
